Clean energy transition

Energy independence is within reach

A transition to clean energy is not just an environmental imperative. It is an opportunity to build a more resilient and equitable economy.

Distributed renewable power reduces exposure to fuel price shocks while keeping investment in local communities. A more diverse grid can respond to disruption without putting every region at risk.

GRID RESILIENCE

DOE 2024

“A diversified portfolio of generation and storage strengthens reliability by reducing the impact of disruptions to any single resource.”

U.S. Department of Energy, National Transmission Needs Study (2024)

Resilient infrastructure protects households first and gives local economies the room to keep moving.

A vault is a folder.
Not a lock-in.

Tagline works with ordinary folders and Markdown files. Notes, speech flows, and canvases live in your vault, so you can keep and back up your work with the tools you already use.

Optional integrations and local models are there when you need them. Your core workspace stays yours.
