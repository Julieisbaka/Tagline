Privacy policy

Tagline is designed around local files and optional connections. This policy explains what happens when you visit this website or use the Tagline desktop app.

Legal

Effective date: November 1, 2026

What this policy covers

This policy applies to the Tagline website, its documentation, and the Tagline desktop app (together, “Tagline”). Tagline is published by the Tagline project. The project does not operate a user account, synchronization, or cloud-storage service.

When you visit this website

The website is made up of static pages. It has no sign-in, contact form, advertising, or analytics scripts, and the site itself does not set cookies. The pages link to services such as GitHub; those services operate independently and may collect information under their own privacy policies.

The site is published using GitHub Pages. Like other web hosts, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and request time to deliver and protect the site. The Tagline project does not control GitHub’s infrastructure or its retention practices. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

When you use the desktop app

Tagline stores your vault files, such as notes and speech materials, in folders on your device. The project does not receive those files through a Tagline-operated cloud service. App settings and downloaded models may be stored in the app’s local profile rather than in your vault. You are responsible for protecting your device and backing up your files.

Some features make network requests when you use them, and file transfers occur only after you confirm them. For example, tournament and caselist features connect to Tabroom or OpenCaselist; local-model features may download model files; and Google Docs transfer sends the selected document to the Google account or webhook you choose after you confirm the transfer. The relevant provider may receive information such as your request, account details, or the content you send, and handles it under its own terms and privacy practices.

For direct Google sign-in, the app requests the permissions described in the Google Docs guide. The guide also explains how credentials are stored locally and how to disconnect. Disconnecting removes the app’s local credentials; it does not itself revoke an authorization grant held by Google.

Messages you send

If you contact the project through GitHub, GitHub receives and hosts your message and account information. Public issues and discussions are visible to others. Do not post passwords, access tokens, private documents, or other sensitive information there.

Your choices and data controls

  • Your local vault files remain under your control; edit, back up, or delete them using your device.
  • Use optional integrations only when you want to, review the destination, and consult the provider’s privacy policy before connecting.
  • Manage or revoke third-party account permissions with the provider. Removing a local app connection may not revoke the provider-side authorization.
  • You can use the website without submitting information to the Tagline project.

Security, retention, and changes

Information stored on your device is subject to your device’s security and backup settings. Information sent to a third party is subject to that provider’s retention and security practices. The project cannot access or delete local files or data held by third parties on your behalf.

We may update this policy as the website or app changes. The effective date above indicates when this version was last updated. Please review the policy periodically, especially before using an optional integration.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project through the Tagline repository on GitHub. That repository is public, so do not include sensitive information in a public issue.
