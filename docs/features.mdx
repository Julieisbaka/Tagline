## Vaults {#vaults}

A vault is a folder on your device containing your notes, flows, and canvases. Your app profile is separate. Learn to [manage and back up your vault](/vaults-and-backups).

## Notes and evidence {#notes}

Write Markdown, format debate cards, and link related research. Start with [notes and evidence](/notes-and-evidence).

## Search and citations {#research}

Find material in your vault or search scholarly metadata, then create citations. See [search and citations](/search-and-citations) and [evidence quality](/evidence-quality).

## Speeches, flows, and canvases {#speech-tools}

Assemble evidence and time your speech with [speech workflows](/speech-workflows), or use [flows and canvases](/flows-and-canvases) to track and connect arguments.

## Import and export {#export}

Move documents with [import and export](/importing-and-exporting) or [Google Docs transfer](/google-docs).

See the [documentation home](/index) for integrations, customization, shortcuts, and developer references.
